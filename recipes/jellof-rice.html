How Nigerian Jellof is Made

Description

Nigerian jellof is perhaps the best jellof in all of Africa. It is relatively easy to prepare and also budget friendly. The Nigerian jellof brings joy to the Nigerian family especially on sundays. if you have never had a Nigerian jellof you are missing alot.

Here i would share with you how i make my jellof rice. Rush to the market get your ingredients and follow these steps

Ingredients

  • Groundnut oil
  • onions
  • Bay leaves
  • Tin tomatoes
  • Fresh Tomatoes
  • Fresh pepper
  • Mixed Spices
  • Ginger and garlic
  • maggi and salt
  • Rice

Steps

  1. perboil your rice half done, rince and drain out the water using a sieve.
  2. wash your put and put the empty pot back on the stove.
  3. Add your oil, when it is hot enough add your oinions.
  4. Add your tintomates into the oil and onions mix and stir for a while.
  5. Add your blended ginger and garlic, add the bay leaves, then add your blended fresh tomatoes and pepper.
  6. Add the mixed spices, maggi and salt for taste. Then add water.
  7. Remove the stew from the pot to a plate and add water to the pot to finish cooking the rice.
  8. When the water boils, add the perboiled rice into the water and pour the stew on the rice.
  9. Add more maggi and salt and stir with a wooden spoon. Check often if you need to add more water, salt or maggi.
  10. Let the rice burn a bit, then stir with a wooden spoon totally, leave on the fire for another 3mins.
  11. your jellof-rice is ready. Serve with any protein of choice.
