Noodles Recipes

Description

Nigerian noodles are a quick, flavorful meal commonly made with instant noodles like Indomie, cooked with spices, vegetables, and sometimes eggs or protein.

It's a fast, satisfying dish loved by both kids and adults. perfect for breakfast, lunch, or a late-night snack.

Ingredients

  • 1 or 2 packs of instant noodles
  • water
  • seasoning from the noodles
  • Oil or butter
  • Chopped onions, pepper, and vegetables (carrot, green pepper, cabbage)
  • Eggs, hotdog or liver

Steps

  1. Boil water and add noodles; cook for 2-3minutes
  2. Add the seasoning and a little oil; stir well
  3. Add vegetables and eggs, hotdogs or liver
  4. Cover and let it cook for another 2 minutes
  5. stir everything together and serve hot
